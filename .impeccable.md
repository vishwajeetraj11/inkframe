# Impeccable Design Context — Inkframe

## Design Context

### Users
Primarily the builder's own audience: visitors evaluating Inkframe as a portfolio/showcase piece. Secondary: creators curious enough to open the editor or template gallery. They arrive cold; the homepage's job is to impress within seconds and route them into the editor/export workflow.

### Brand Personality
Confident, kinetic, editorial. Three words: **bold, precise, cinematic**. The product makes Vox-style editorial motion graphics — the brand should feel like a title sequence, not a SaaS pitch.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Bold kinetic typography on dark.** The homepage behaves like a cinematic title sequence: the type IS the interface. Huge condensed uppercase headlines (Barlow Condensed) with hard-cut word cycling, contrasted with Cormorant Garamond italic moments and IBM Plex Mono timecode details. Minimal chrome — full-width rows and hairlines instead of cards.

- Theme: dark, warm ink (`#0F0D0A` family) — never the generic cyan-on-navy AI palette
- Type: bone white (`#F2EDE3`); one sharp accent, vermillion `#FF4F1F` (a tally/record light)
- Anti-references: glassmorphism cards, glow blobs, purple-blue gradients, identical card grids, gradient text

### Design Principles
1. **Type is the interface** — typography carries hierarchy, motion, and personality; chrome is minimal.
2. **Hard cuts, smooth decelerations** — motion mimics editing: staggered reveals with ease-out-expo, word swaps as cuts. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
3. **One accent, used like a tally light** — vermillion appears sparingly and means "live/active".
4. **Editorial texture, not decoration** — timecode, frame counts, preset names as real data; no sparklines or fake UI.
5. **Rows and rules over cards** — full-bleed rows, hairline dividers, asymmetric composition.
